Shared Dining Strategy

How We’ll Eat Within Budget

Core Rule: Save on routine meals so we can spend on experiences and the meals that actually matter.

Morning
Easy & Inexpensive. Included hotel breakfast (Grotta, Quirinale, Vila Galé) or pastries, coffee, yogurt, fruit, and apartment breakfasts. Café breakfasts only when the setting warrants it.
Lunch
Main Savings Lever. Bakeries, baguettes, sandwiches, gyros, pizza, markets, beach food, picnics, or apartment bites. Avoid destination restaurants at both lunch and dinner.
Afternoon
Flexible. Coffee, aperitivo, beach drinks, terrace wine, or casual cocktails.
Dinner
Spend Selectively. Atmospheric local tavernas / konobas / bistros, distinctive neighborhood spots, regional specialties, plus limited high-impact special dinners mixed with casual favorites.
